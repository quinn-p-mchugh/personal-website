- Go to https://www.goodreads.com/review/import
- Export your books - Under "Export", click "Export Library"
- Wait for the export to display
- ![alt text](Website/blog-micro%20(2)/2024/goodreads-my-books-import-export-page.png)
- Download the export and open in Excel or Google Sheets
- Insert a new column next to the "Author" column (name it something like "Author URL")
