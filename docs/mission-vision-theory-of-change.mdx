## Mission

I believe solving today's [global systemic issues](https://www.artberman.com/blog/metacrisis-getting-honest-about-the-human-predicament/), such as [climate change](https://80000hours.org/problem-profiles/climate-change/) and [intensive animal farming](https://80000hours.org/problem-profiles/factory-farming/), hinges on our ability to foster & harness collective intelligence,<sup>[\[1\]](https://www.nesta.org.uk/toolkit/collective-intelligence-design-playbook/) [\[2\]](https://youtu.be/PLCLUonwMdo?si=hzfRZ_gRCsVC02GC) [\[3\]](https://www.dataone.org/about/)</sup> enable large-scale human cooperation, deliberation, & sense-making,<sup>[\[1\]](https://www.peoplepowered.org/platform-ratings) [\[2\]](https://kumu.io/gallery) [\[3\]](https://electionscience.org/)</sup> and drive bold institutional reform.<sup>[\[1\]](https://effectiveinstitutionsproject.org/)</sup>

My mission is to help individuals, organizations, and institutions to create resilient systems that incentivize prosocial behavior<sup>[\[1\]](https://youtu.be/tf-2ycHHgtI?si=Nfe5roY7UZc9i1Nz) [\[2\]](https://www.plurality.net/)</sup>, support the common good,<sup>[\[1\]](https://www.fundingthecommons.io/)</sup> and improve the welfare of [all sentient beings](https://www.sentienceinstitute.org/perspective#moral-circle-expansion).
